How the model works +

Scope: one HCP-performed index test per acute episode in a hospital pathway, plus entered extra kits. All eligible admissions are within those tested episodes. It explores shorter occupied stays; it does not model prevented hospital attendance, diagnosis or treatment decisions.

Monthly cost = kits × (kit price + additional paid minutes ÷ 60 × loaded hourly cost) + extra follow-up + recurring costs + setup ÷ pilot months.

Required bed-days = monthly cost ÷ resource value per occupied bed-day. The headline rounds up to a whole bed-day. Other outputs use unrounded values.

Scenario capacity = eligible admissions × assumed reduction in occupied days. Resource value and cash avoided use separate entered unit values. Cash is a component of resource value; never add the two. Extra paid time is a cost; redeployed staff time should be measured as workload separately.

Limits: constant monthly activity, no discounting, no downstream patient outcomes, no causal estimate, no probability model, no formal cost-effectiveness claim. The shorter-stay hypothesis itself needs evaluation. A favourable result cannot substitute for clinical safety, regulatory clearance or an NHS procurement decision.
